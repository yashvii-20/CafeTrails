  • Key Features and Highlights:

  • Atmosphere & Ambience:

    • Triton Café delivers a modern, vibrant ambience with a design that blends contemporary interiors and subtle luxury. It’s known for being stylish yet approachable, offering a laid-back yet Instagram-friendly setting.

  • Cuisine:

    • A diverse, all‑vegetarian menu features Asian, North‑Indian, Mexican, Italian, and even Greek‑style dishes. Costs are typically around ₹1,100–1,500 for two depending on whether you order lighter bites or a full multi‑cuisine meal . The menu spans ~15 online pages, indicating extensive choices .

  • Recommended Dishes:

    • Asian & Chinese Starters. Mushroom Dim Sum – Often praised for its perfection and photogenic appeal.
    • Mexican/Continental Bites. Loaded Nachos/Tacos – Crisp and colorful, go-to options for a casual bite Avocado & Mango Salsa – Fresh, tangy, and perfect with chips
    • Drinks & Mocktails Santorini-style Refreshments – Expect photogenic mocktails perfect for pairing with the ambiance
    • Desserts & Quick Bites Waffles or Cakes – Light, sweet treats to complete the meal Ice Cream & Shakes – Locally popular for dessert lovers

  • Location:

    • TP‑213, FP‑39, Sur‑88/2/1, near Sindhu Bhavan Road, opposite AUDA Garden & Swastik School, PRL Colony, Bodakdev, Ahmedabad, Gujarat 380054, India

  • Ratings & Reviews:

    • Restaurant Guru reports a rating of 4.3/5 (based on 1,080+ reviews)
    • On Google, it averages 4.3–4.4 stars, while Zomato shows a lower 3.2/5 from around 85 ratings
    • Hungrito lists it among “Top 10 Cafes in Ahmedabad 2025”, highlighting its ambiance and “Greece vibes” as perfect for Instagram‑worthy moments

  • Overall:

    • “Triton Café offers a stylish, chill vibe with a modern café-lounge atmosphere—ideal for those who enjoy classy interiors, calm music, and a place to relax or socialize comfortably.”

  • Parking:

    • Provides free parking lot, street parking, and valet parking options.
