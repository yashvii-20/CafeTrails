...Banjara Gourmet Cafe...

  • Key Features and Highlights:

  • Atmosphere & Ambience:

    • Banjara Gourmet Cafe embraces a rustic-meets-boho vibe, creating a peaceful and artsy atmosphere. The ambience is rich with natural elements—think wooden furniture, rattan lights, indoor plants, and cane decor, giving it an eco-conscious, grounded look. Stylish and spacious indoor and outdoor setting, often described as romantic or upscale, with live music arrangements.

  • Cuisine:

    • All-vegetarian fine-casual dining, featuring Continental, North Indian, Chinese, Oriental, Italian, and desserts.

  • Recommended Dishes:

    • According to diners and local guides, the must-try vegetarian dishes include:
    • Burmese Khao Suey, Manchow soup
    • chilli bao, dim sums, Hong Kong noodles
    • Rainbow & Margherita pizzas, carrot halwa cake, cheesecakes
    • Dal Bukhara, Kadai Paneer, Tibetan Thukpa soup, cheese croquettes

  • Location:
