...House Of Makeba...

  • Key Features and Highlights:

  • Atmosphere & Ambience:

    • The standout feature of Makeba Cafe at Bhat is its rooftop setting with sweeping views of the skyline and Sardar Patel Ring Road. As the sun sets, the space transforms into a romantic, breezy spot ideal for evening hangouts or dates.Makeba’s decor is a blend of bohemian charm and modern minimalism.

  • Cuisine:

    • An all-vegetarian fine-casual restaurant and terrace lounge, serving Indian, Mexican, Italian, Chinese, Continental, and more.

  • Recommended Dishes:

    • Soups & Salads Khowsuey Soup: Creamy coconut-based, loaded with toppings—rich and comforting Makeba Energy Bowl: Quinoa, pomegranate, nuts, berries—fresh yet filling Chilli Corn Avocado Salad: A vibrant, tangy mix with avocado, corn, cherry tomatoes—perfect starter
    • Noodles, Pizza & Rice Dan Dan or Drunken Noodles: Choose chili-oil spicy or basil-garlic saucy versions Veggie Fiesta Pizza: Thin crust topped with fresh veggies—a crowd favorite Veg Alfredo Pasta: Creamy and rich—great for comfort food lovers Peri-Peri Cream Cheese Rice Bowl: Zesty and creamy, hits the spicy-savory combo

  • Location:

    • 2, Sigma 1, Corporate House, Sindhu Bhavan Road, Opposite Man Party Plot, Bodakdev, Ahmedabad, Gujarat-380054, India
