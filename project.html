...The Project Cafe (Yellow House)...

  • Key Features and Highlights:

  • Atmosphere & Ambience:

    • Minimal décor—white plastered walls, textured curves, metal shelving—serve as a neutral backdrop for bold, colourful art and furniture Described as having a “Santorini hut‑like” feel with white interiors and a bright yellow facade .

  • Cuisine:

    • Extensive menu featuring Italian pizzas and pastas, Lebanese mezze, Mexican bowls, Thai delights, and local creations Offers a wide range of vegetarian and vegan options, including salads, sandwiches, and desserts

  • Recommended Dishes:

    • Blueberry Cheesecake – A popular indulgence praised by many
    • Classic or Nutella Hot Chocolate / Dark Almond Shake – Comforting and rich treat

    • Tripadvisor-One perfect 5/5 review praising service, food, and ambience—but limited sample size.

    • The Project Café is more than just a place to eat—it’s a cultural microcosm. Whether you're a food lover, an art enthusiast, or seeking a unique space to unwind, it delivers: Artistic Immersion: Changing exhibitions and interactive design pieces make every visit fresh and inspiring.
